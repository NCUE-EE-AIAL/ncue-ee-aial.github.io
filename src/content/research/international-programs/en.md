Each year the lab hosts international students through two Taiwan government programs: [TEEP](https://teep.studyintaiwan.org/) (Taiwan Experience Education Program, Ministry of Education; the lab's Taiwan–India project since 2018) and [IIPP](https://iipp.stpi.niar.org.tw/) (International Internship Pilot Program, National Science and Technology Council; since 2024). <!-- source: Prof. Yang's IIPP mentor page https://iipp.stpi.niar.org.tw/mentor/846 (checked 2026-10-17) --> <!-- TODO: confirm with lab: partner universities --> These students arrive with different backgrounds, and the lab gives them a concrete project in which to apply AI to a real problem.

Projects span signal processing, time-series prediction, robotics and computer vision, typically built with Python notebooks and low-cost embedded hardware such as Arduino and ESP boards. Several have grown into collaborations or papers, for example the two-step authentication work presented at ICETA 2024.

**What interns do.** Typical tasks are digital signal processing (short-time Fourier transform, discrete wavelet transform, digital filtering), machine learning with LSTM and general regression neural networks, simulation and analysis in MATLAB and Python, and implementing them on embedded hardware such as FPGA boards. Interns report weekly and meet the group every week; C or C++ programming is expected. <!-- source: Prof. Yang's TEEP 2023 and IIPP 2024 slides, provided by Brian on 2026-10-18 -->

**Topics offered.** Recent calls offered smart grids and power quality, and wavelet neural networks for the industrial internet of things (IIoT), both covering feature extraction, system-response identification and system-status identification; embedded image recognition (feature extraction, contour identification, object recognition); and AI and edge computing for the campus battery energy storage system.

Representative projects include the [Embedded Wavelet Neural Network for ECG Analysis](https://github.com/NCUE-EE-AIAL/Embedded-Wavelet-Neural-Network-for-ECG-Analysis), [NCUE BEES energy consumption analysis and prediction](https://github.com/NCUE-EE-AIAL/NCUE-BEES-Energy-Consumption-analysis-and-prediction) and [PatrolBot](https://github.com/NCUE-EE-AIAL/PatrolBot).
