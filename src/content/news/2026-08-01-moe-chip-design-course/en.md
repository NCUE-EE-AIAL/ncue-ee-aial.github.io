Prof. Wen-Ren Yang is a co-principal investigator of the Ministry of Education's 跨域智慧晶片設計課程推廣計畫 (Cross-Domain Smart Chip Design Course Promotion Program; the English name is our translation) for academic year 2026–27, running from August 2026 to July 2027.

The program promotes cross-domain courses in smart chip design. It fits the lab's hands-on work with FPGA boards and edge-AI modules, listed under facilities on the [Join](/join/) page.
