---

Tingyi Lin, Prof. Wen-Ren Yang and Kuanwei Chen posted "Emotion as a Distribution: Joint Valence-Arousal Probability Learning for Speaker-Independent Multimodal Emotion Recognition" on arXiv ([arXiv:2609.05755](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05755)); it has been submitted to *Speech Communication*.

Instead of assigning one emotion label to an utterance, the model predicts a joint probability distribution over valence and arousal from text and audio. The work compares bidirectional Mamba state-space models with Transformer backbones on IEMOCAP. The code is public as [EchoMind-Mamba-VA-SER](https://github.com/brian10420/EchoMind-Mamba-VA-SER).
