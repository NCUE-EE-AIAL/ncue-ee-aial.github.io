Muskan is a PhD scholar in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Presidency University Bangalore, India, and is spending a semester at National Changhua University of Education on an exchange program. Presidency University featured her in a [student spotlight on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/presidency-university-india_communicationskills-studentspotlight-presidencyuniversity-activity-7453335319992766464-HnhV).

In the video she talks about the research facilities and new technologies she has worked with, the collaborative academic environment, and what she has learned from meeting students from many countries. You can also read her [testimonial on our International Programs page](/research/international-programs/#testimonial-muskan).
