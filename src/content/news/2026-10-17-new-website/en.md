The AI Application Laboratory has launched a new bilingual website covering our research areas, projects, publications, people and news. The previous site was hosted on GitHub Pages under the ncue-ee-aial organisation. All content on the new site lives in plain files in the lab's GitHub repository, so lab members can contribute by editing those files and opening a pull request.
